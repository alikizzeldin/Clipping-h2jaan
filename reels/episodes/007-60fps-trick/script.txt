GTA 6 is stuck at 30 FPS on every console… even the PS5 Pro. But there's a trick that makes it look like 60. Run your PS5 through a capture card into your PC, and switch off HDCP on the PS5. Open the feed at 30 FPS, then open Lossless Scaling, a seven-dollar app. Frame generation: LSFG, Fixed, times two. And thirty… becomes sixty. The catch? It still feels like 30 in your hands. No capture card? Remote Play works too, with more lag.
