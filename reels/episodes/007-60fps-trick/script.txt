GTA 6 is stuck at 30 FPS on every console… even the PS5 Pro. But there's a trick that makes it look like 60… and a free way at the end. Run your PS5 through a capture card into your PC, and switch off HDCP on the PS5. Then open Lossless Scaling, a seven-dollar app. Frame generation: LSFG, Fixed, times two. And thirty… becomes sixty. The catch? It still feels like 30 in your hands. Now the free way. This app is free and open source, has frame generation built in… and can even stream your PS5 over Remote Play, no capture card needed.
