GTA 6 hides a Mario block… and it drops Mario hats. Go to Lummox County, to the ruined paper mill on the river in south Moreau. By the water wheel, use the bong. While your character trips, look up: a see-through block floats over the ruins. Jump into it, like Mario. Two caps drop out: a J for Jason and an L for Lucia. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
