GTA 6 hides a secret bunker… under an RV. First, finish the Chapter 1 mission Building a Life. Then head southwest of Griffin Shooting Range in Balsero, to a fenced junkyard full of old trailers. Find the RV and step inside. There's a hatch in the kitchen floor. Open it and climb down the ladder. Waiting at the bottom: a free Conspiracist Type 9 machine pistol. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
