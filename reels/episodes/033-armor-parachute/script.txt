Stop jumping with armor on in GTA 6.
Here's why.
Hold L1 for the wheel, then switch to the Items tab.
Select the vest, and a blue bar shows above your health.
But armor and parachute share the same slot.
Only one can be on at a time.
So before you leap off a roof or a chopper, press R2 on that slot to swap to the chute.
Then jump.
