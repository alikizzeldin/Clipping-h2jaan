GTA 6 has its own X-Men… and the villain is a pill. Go to the Twenty Four Seven store in Key Lento and check the magazine rack. There's a comic called RX-Men. The X became R-X, like on a prescription. Their enemy is Doctor Placebo, a villain with a pill-capsule head who wants to wipe out medicine. And the heroes fight back with big pharma powers. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
