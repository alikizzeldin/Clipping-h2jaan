Stop smashing car windows in GTA 6… it's costing you money. When you steal a car, you get two options: Slim Jim, or Smash Window. Smashing is faster, but a broken window counts as damage, and fences pay less for damaged cars. The Slim Jim takes a few seconds longer, but leaves the car intact. And skip cars with a tracker… they're worth even less. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
