GTA 6 has no mission replay… so save before it's too late. Unlike GTA 5, once a story mission is done, you can't play it again. And you can't save in the middle of one. You get fifteen manual save slots, plus one autosave. So before every chapter finale, save to a fresh slot. Do it at the start and end of each chapter, and you can always go back.
