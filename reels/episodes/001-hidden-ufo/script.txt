GTA 6 has a hidden UFO… and it can take you. Go to the dirt road east of Port Gellhorn, near the Starlet Motel. Wait until after 2 AM, in-game time. A woman caught in a beam of light will ask you to get her car. Go get it… and the UFO comes back for you instead. You wake up three days later, with a secret trophy: The Truth Is Out There. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
