Bought GTA 6's Ultimate Edition? One bonus only works in Chapter 1. It's a free Retro Build for Jason's pickup, the Vapid Ganado. First, finish the mission Just a Scratch. Then open your map, go to the Vehicles index, and find Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. In the mod menu, open Pro Builds. The Retro Build is free there. But once Chapter 1 ends, it's gone.
