GTA 6 hides a free sports car in Chapter 1… but it disappears forever. Jason's neighbor Shirley, in Key Lento, has two optional favors. First, get Shirley's rum. Later, she calls you over again: steal her neighbor Peggy's scooter. Do both, and Shirley lets you use her Penumbra whenever you want. But both favors are gone for good once Tourist Trap starts. So do them first. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
