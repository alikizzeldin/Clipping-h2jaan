GTA 6 has no first-person mode… but there's a hidden view. Standing still, hold L2 and click R3: you're looking through your character's eyes. You can't walk like this, but it's perfect for screenshots. With a scoped gun, do the same while aiming. And in a car, press up on the D-pad to pull out your phone: now you're driving in first person. Just don't crash while scrolling. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
