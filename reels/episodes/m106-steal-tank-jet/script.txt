You can steal a tank in GTA 6… and a fighter jet. Head to Mile Zero Air Station, the military base at Leonida's southwest edge. It's open from Chapter 1. Skip the gate: the stunt jump on the southeast side throws you over the fence. Fighter jets are parked in the north. The tanks wait in the south. Grab a Rhino, drive it and fire the cannon… and a secret trophy pops: Challenging Tank. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
