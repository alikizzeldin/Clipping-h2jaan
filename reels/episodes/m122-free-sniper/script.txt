GTA 6 hides a free sniper rifle… near Jason's house. In Key Lento, find the clothing store Nifty Threads. Right next to it stands a tall gray tower. Climb over the chain-link gate, go through the gray door at the base, and take the ladder all the way up. On the balcony at the top: the Shrewsbury SM-69. At Ammu-Nation, it costs eighteen hundred and fifty dollars. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
