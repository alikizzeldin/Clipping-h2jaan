You can get fat in GTA 6… sort of. Eat fast food until your Fitness app shows a calorie surplus, then sleep. Repeat for a few days, skip your workouts, and Jason or Lucia gets heavier. Want to bulk up instead? Lift at the free weights behind Jason's house: Strength raises your max health. The catch? Even maxed out, they're nowhere near as big as CJ.
