Rockstar hid Arthur Morgan in GTA 6… twice. First, his hat. In Chapter 2, find the yard sale in the south of Port Gellhorn. It sells a cowboy hat that copies Arthur's, right down to the rope knot. If the yard is empty, come back later. Then drive to Frontier Adventure Golf in Waning Sands. Walk in and watch the chihuahua roaming the course… a tiny Arthur Morgan is riding it.
