Stop selling your stolen cars at Pay 'n' Spray for a few thousand dollars. Halfway through Chapter 3, after you steal a car for Randy, his Wish List unlocks. Bring him a car from the list in perfect condition and he pays thirty thousand dollars or more. Every scratch lowers the price, so drive carefully. With sixteen to eighteen cars on the list, that's about half a million dollars. Follow for a new GTA 6 secret every day.
